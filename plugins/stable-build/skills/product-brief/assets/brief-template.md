# Product brief template

A flexible starting structure. Drop sections that do not earn their place, add what the product needs, reorder freely.

```markdown
---
title: {Product name}
status: draft
created: {YYYY-MM-DD}
updated: {YYYY-MM-DD}
---

# Product brief: {Product name}

## Summary
[2-3 paragraphs: what this is, what problem it solves, why it matters, why now. Someone who reads only this section understands it.]

## The problem
[Who feels the pain, how they cope today, what the status quo costs them. Real scenarios.]

## The solution
[What is being built and how it solves the problem. The experience and the outcome, not the implementation.]

## What makes this different
[Why this approach over alternatives. Be honest: if the edge is speed of execution, say so.]

## Who this serves
[Primary users: who they are, what they need, what success looks like for them.]

## Onchain angle
[Which Arc building blocks it uses (for example USDC payments, transaction memos, batched transfers, crosschain transfers), each confirmed live on docs.arc.io with its URL. Wallet model (EOA or smart account). Network plan: Arc Testnet first, what must be true before mainnet. What becomes public onchain (amounts, addresses, memos) and what stays offchain. If an idea came from Arc's Request for Builders, name the frontier and link it.]

## Success criteria
[How we know it works: user signals and product goals, measurable.]

## Scope
[In for the first version; explicitly out. A boundary, not a feature list.]

## Open questions
[What is still unknown, with an owner.]

## Vision
[Where it goes if it works. Grounded.]
```
